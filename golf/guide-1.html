Overhead, behind or beside: choosing a launch monitor

← All buying guides

2 minute read · Prices checked October 28, 2026

Choose where the monitor sits before you choose the brand. The mounting position decides how much room you need, whether left- and right-handed golfers can share the room easily, and whether the monitor can leave the house. Price and software come after that.

The three positions

  • Overhead (ceiling): nothing on the floor and no moving it between golfers. It suits a dedicated room with enough height. Examples: Uneekor EYE XO and XO2, Trackman iO.
  • Behind the golfer (floor): radar units that read the whole ball flight. They need depth behind and in front, and many are portable. Examples: Trackman 4, FlightScope Mevo+.
  • Beside the ball (floor): camera units placed next to the hitting area. Compact and often portable, but they move sides when a left-handed golfer plays. Examples: Uneekor EYE MINI LITE, SkyTrak ST MAX, Foresight GCQuad.

A launch monitor is one part of the room. A projector such as the BenQ AH700ST was listed at $2,299 at Indoor Golf Outlet, and the screen, enclosure, mat and computer come on top. For the space each position needs, read How much room a home golf simulator needs.

Software and computer

  • Uneekor's simulation software needs a supported Windows PC; a Mac is not a validated setup.
  • GSPro, used by several monitors, is a third-party subscription. Confirm the current price and supported devices.
  • Trackman runs its own software. Mevo+ editions differ in which course software is included, so match the exact package.

Matching the monitor to you

  • Dedicated room, mixed-handed household: an overhead monitor.
  • Shared or flexible space, or play at the range too: a portable unit behind the golfer.
  • Smaller room, one golfer: a unit beside the ball is the most compact.
