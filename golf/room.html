The field guide

Before you choose

The questions to settle before ordering.

  1. 01Allow cue room. For an 8-foot table, Pooltables.com recommends a room of about 13 ft 1 in by 16 ft 6 in with 48-inch cues, up to 13 ft 11 in by 17 ft 4 in with standard 58-inch cues.
  2. 02Add installation to the price. These listings ship curbside by default; white-glove delivery and installation is an add-on of $699 to $1,099, and the seller notes that improper installation may void the warranty.
  3. 03Check the floor and the route. The Gold Crown VII weighs 1,417 lb and the Parsons shuffleboard 661 lb; measure doorways, stairs and turns, and choose a two-piece shuffleboard playfield for tight rooms.
  4. 04Ask about timing. Several of these tables were listed as shipping in mid-December when we checked.
