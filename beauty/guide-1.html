Fragrance-free or scented skincare: reading the label

← All buying guides

1 minute read · Facts checked October 28, 2026

Fragrance-free and unscented are different promises. Fragrance-free means no fragrance was added. Unscented means the product should not smell of much, which can be achieved by adding a masking fragrance. If you prefer to avoid fragrance, look for fragrance-free and read the ingredient list.

How to read the label

  • Look for "fragrance" or "parfum" in the ingredient list.
  • Essential oils add scent too. A product can be free of synthetic fragrance and still be scented with citrus, lavender or jasmine oils.
  • Patch test anything new on a small area first, and ask a dermatologist if your skin reacts easily.
