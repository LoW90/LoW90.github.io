Choosing a robot mower by lawn size and slope

Nov 4, 2026

← All buying guides

2 minute read · Manufacturer specifications checked October 28, 2026

Two numbers rule out most robot mowers before anything else: the area of the lawn and its steepest slope. Start with those, then check positioning under trees and narrow passages. A mower rated for steep ground may still be rated for less area, so compare both numbers together.

Measure first

  • Area: your mowed lawn only, not the whole lot. An acre is 43,560 sq ft. The house, drive, patio and beds all come off the lot size.
  • Slope: a 100% slope is a 45-degree incline, and 40% is about 22 degrees. A phone level app on a straight board gives a usable reading at the steepest spot.
  • Sky view: wire-free mowers use satellite positioning, cameras or LiDAR. Dense tree cover and tall walls can weaken positioning, so note those areas.

Before you order

  1. Choose a mower rated for more area than you have; maker ratings assume good conditions.
  2. Ask whether mapping, positioning or anti-theft features need a subscription.
  3. Check that the narrowest passage between lawn zones is wide enough for the mower.
  4. Plan where the dock goes: it needs power and a level spot within the positioning signal.
