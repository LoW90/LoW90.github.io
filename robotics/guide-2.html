Robot pool cleaners: what "pool size" really means

Nov 4, 2026

← All buying guides

2 minute read · Manufacturer specifications checked October 28, 2026

Pool cleaners are rated in square feet, but the makers do not all measure the same thing. One figure can be the area cleaned in a single cycle. Another can be the largest pool supported, or the area for one cleaning mode. Compare like with like, then choose by which surfaces you want cleaned.

Three different numbers

  • Single-cycle coverage: how much the robot cleans on one charge or run.
  • Maximum pool size: the largest pool the maker supports, which may take more than one cycle.
  • Mode coverage: the area for one mode, such as floor only or waterline only.

Beatbot, for example, gives 3,444 sq ft for one cycle of the AquaSense 2 Ultra in its FAQ, and a larger total pool area in its marketing. Both are correct; they answer different questions.

How to choose

  • Leaves and pollen on the surface: choose a model that skims, such as the AquaSense 2 Ultra or the Scuba X1 Pro Max.

Before you order

  1. Measure the water surface. A 20 × 40 ft pool is 800 sq ft; steps, a deep end and a spa add complexity.
  2. Confirm your pool's finish is on the maker's list. Aiper lists concrete, fiberglass, vinyl and tile for the Scuba X1 Pro Max.
  3. On a saltwater pool, check the salt limit in the manual.
  4. Ask about replacement filters, batteries and the warranty length.
