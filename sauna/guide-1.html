What a home sauna really costs, installed

← All buying guides

2 minute read · Prices checked October 20, 2026

The listed price is for the sauna shell. The finished project usually costs more. In the listings we checked, the heater was sold separately, delivery terms differed by retailer, and the electrical work, foundation and permits were never included. Here is what to add, and what to ask each seller.

The line items

Line itemWhat we foundWhat to ask
Sauna shell$4,990 (X2 indoor) to $9,490 (GL4 cabin) for the four SaunaLife modelsIs this exact configuration in stock, and when would it ship?
Heater and controlsSold separately in the Topture listings, from $1,254Which heater is matched to this room, and what circuit does it need?
DeliveryTopture's E7 listing mentions a $950 freight line. Select Saunas advertises free shipping on most saunas, with exclusionsIs curbside or placed delivery included? Who offloads it?
ElectricalNot included anywhereA licensed electrician's quote for a dedicated circuit and an inspection
Foundation or room preparationNot includedA level pad or slab outdoors, or flooring and ventilation indoors
PermitsDepends on where you liveYour local building department's rules on setbacks and outdoor structures
AssemblyVaries by modelIs assembly included, and roughly how long does it take?

Comparing two quotes fairly

Two sellers can quote the same model with a different heater, a different delivery level or a different bundle. Compare them only after the configuration matches line by line. Our model pages show each seller's terms side by side for this reason.

The SaunaLife E7 ERGO barrel is listed at both Select Saunas and Topture, with different delivery terms. The GL4 glass-front cabin price is before the heater.

A simple rule of thumb

For an outdoor sauna, treat the shell price as the start of the budget, then get the electrician's and the site quotes before you order. For an indoor room, the electrician's visit comes first: the panel's spare capacity decides which heaters are possible. Still choosing a placement?
