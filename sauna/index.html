The field guide

Build well. Live beautifully.

A premium sauna is an architectural decision as well as a wellness purchase. These are the questions to settle before ordering.

01

Choose your heat.

Traditional electric and wood-fired saunas produce different experiences from infrared cabins. Heater specification can materially change a package price.

02

Plan the site.

Consider electrical capacity, drainage, weather exposure, setbacks and the exact delivery route before commissioning the build.

03

Know the real cost.

Equipment prices can exclude the heater, permits, electrical work, foundation, shipping, assembly and finish work.

The thoughtful investment

Your complete project, considered.

Start with the equipment, then model the work around it. This local worksheet uses only your inputs; the sample values are illustrative and are not contractor quotes.

Does the listed sauna price include a heater?

Not necessarily. Several SaunaLife sauna shell listings require a separately selected heater package. Confirm the exact kit with the merchant.

What should I ask before ordering?

Ask for a written quote including heater, control, stones, shipping, offloading, electrical, site preparation, taxes, warranty and return restrictions.

Are permits and professional installation necessary?

Requirements vary by municipality, electrical system, structure and site. A qualified local electrician and permitting office should confirm before purchase.
