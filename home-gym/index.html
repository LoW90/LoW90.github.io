The field guide

Before you choose

The questions to settle before ordering.

  1. 01Measure the room before you choose. The Run Personal needs about 79 by 31 inches of floor; the Kinesis Personal needs a 67-inch wall and Technogym lists 79 inches of frontage for use.
  2. 02Ask what installation includes. Technogym's prices for the Run Personal and Kinesis Personal include delivery and installation; Tonal charges installation separately.
  3. 03Count the running costs. Tonal needs a membership and an accessory bundle on top of the machine price.
  4. 04Check the floor. Heavy equipment on an upper floor may need a structural check; ask the seller for the machine's weight.
