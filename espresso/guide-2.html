Why the grinder matters as much as the espresso machine

← All buying guides

1 minute read · Facts checked October 28, 2026

If you choose a hands-on machine, budget for a grinder at the same time. The Breville Bambino Plus and the Rancilio Silvia Pro X both need a separate grinder. Espresso asks for a fine, even grind that you can adjust in very small steps, and pre-ground coffee loses its aroma quickly.

What to look for

  • Fine, stepless or small-step adjustment. Espresso is sensitive to tiny changes in grind size.
  • Little coffee left behind. Less retention means each dose is fresher and you waste fewer beans.
  • Height under your cabinets. Grinders with a bean hopper on top can be taller than the machine.
  • Quiet operation, if you make coffee while others are asleep.

When you do not need one

Machines with a built-in grinder, such as the Breville Barista Express and every automatic machine in our catalog, already grind for you. If you would rather not choose two products, start with Automatic, assisted or hands-on: choosing an espresso machine.
