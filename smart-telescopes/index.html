The field guide

Before you choose

The questions to settle before ordering.

  1. 01Eyepiece or screen. The Pro models and the eVscope 2 add a Nikon-developed electronic eyepiece; the others show the view on a phone or tablet.
  2. 02Weigh it before you carry it. The Odyssey models weigh about 4 kg; the eQuinox 2 and eVscope 2 about 7 kg.
  3. 03Battery for a long night. From about 5 hours (Odyssey) to 11 hours (eQuinox 2).
  4. 04Returns and warranty. Unistellar lists a 30-day return policy and a 2-year warranty on the Expert range.
